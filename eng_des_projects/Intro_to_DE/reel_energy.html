REEL ENERGY

A BLUE SKY CONCEPT, TO TAKE ADVANTAGE OF WASTED ENERGY FROM THE MOST UNEXPECTED OF PLACES

BRIEF

In my first term of Design Engineering at Imperial College London, I was tasked with analysing energy in the home, in particular where it is lost, and how this can be reused. Blue sky thinking was then applied, to develop an ambitious concept.It began with many stages of ideation, leading to these three ideas.

CABLE ORGANISER

The cable organiser was selected. It organises your cables on your desk, whilst also harvesting energy once they are pulled for use.

ITERATION

Aspects such as shape, form and mechanism were explored.

FORMING REQUIREMENTS

This graphic I created as one of my first endeavors on adobe illustrator, to represent the wy the developed version of the product works. There is now a battery to store harvested energy, which can be removed.

CONCEPT

The final result was a unit which sits on you desk and organises your cables. However, the way the cables are organised mean that when they are pulled to be used, a turbine is spun and energy is generated and collected in a battery pack, which can then be removed and used.
