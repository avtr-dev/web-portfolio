SPOT DATING

Meet People Over Common Interests

CONCEPT

Spot is a dating app concept that revolves around common interests. Matching people on an event that they wish to attend provides a meaningful experience and entertaining plan for a first date. Spot eliminates static dating profiles by creating event specific prompts that allow couples to engage in interesting and unique discussions. Hive partners with event spaces to create a scalable business model that can be implemented in any city.

UI/UX Design

I worked with the team on the UI/UX concepts for the app. These along with wireframe mockups were created on Figma.

BRANDING

I led the branding for this project, conducting ideation on adobe illustrator.
