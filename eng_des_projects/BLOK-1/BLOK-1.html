BLOK-1

A three dimensional score and beat builder that redefines conventional music notation and creation

CONCEPT

A tangible grid based block beat builder for drums, piano and other instruments. Designed to rapidly build riffs, create funky tunes and methodologically build up beats. Produced as a result of a large research phase, where it was identified that many people who live with learning differences struggle to play music through traditional methods. We adopted the brand of teenage engineering for this product.

SHEET MUSIC RE-IMAGINED

Despite being an invaluable and playful resource to all musicians for rapid score prototyping, a key pain point which is solved, is the creation of an alternative to sheet music. This is done through the block designs, which through tactility, imagery and colour represent and function like the notes used in traditional sheet music.

INDUSTRIAL DESIGN

Some areas which I undertook completely on my own in this group project were the DFMA considerations, as well as all of the CAD, rendering and animation. I created a full data production package, outlining how this product would be industrially manufactured.

KEYSHOT AND SOLIDWORKS

The skill which I developed furthest, was most likely my Solidworks CAD, and Keyshot rendering. I produced all renders for the project deliverables. The toughest elements were achieving the pearlescent LED diffusion acrylic material and creating an assembly with over 250 individual components.

TECHNOLOGY STACK

I assisted our lead electronics and programming member to perfect the technology stack, through which I learnt MAX MSP, an audio processing software, as well as developing my arduino and electronics abilities greatly.

THE BLOCK READING

A key requirement of the product, is to identify where on the grid blocks are being placed. This was achieved through giving each block a different resistance, and then when they are placed on the grid, the circuit is completed through the array of bolts.

PACKAGING

I designed and did CAD and renders for a set of packaging which follows all product labelling regulations as well as fitting in with existing brand guidelines of teenage engineering.

APP INTEGRATION

This product aims to have mass market impact whilst also aiding those with learning difficulties. The potential to grow the community through sharing and sending of user created beats is just the beginning of what app integration could add to this product.

FINAL PROTOTYPE

After months of development and a rush of soldering and troubleshooting, a fully working prototype was created and shown off in a demo and presentation.
